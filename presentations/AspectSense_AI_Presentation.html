1. Problem Statement & Motivation

❌ Traditional Sentiment Analysis

Assigns a single document-level label (e.g. Positive or Negative). Masks critical product flaws when a customer praises one feature while condemning another.

"Stunning screen, but battery drains in 3 hours." → Misclassified as Neutral!

✅ AspectSense Solution

Deconstructs customer discourse into discrete feature aspects, scores localized polarities, and uses Gemini 3.8 Flash LLM to diagnose engineering root causes.

  • Clause segmentation
  • Multi-aspect ontology matching
  • Automated empathetic support drafting

3. Key Features & Capabilities

🎯 Fine-Grained ABSA Engine

Extracts features across Smartphones, Laptops, Audio, Cloud SaaS, and Hospitality. Maps synonyms (e.g., OLED, AMOLED, 120Hz → screen_display).

🤖 Contextual LLM Reasoning

Goes beyond classification to answer why the defect happened and what actions engineering and QA must take to prevent recurrences.

🚨 Urgency & Emotion Triaging

Automatically flags Critical tickets (outages, safety hazards, refund threats) to cut customer support SLA turnaround times.

🛡️ Graceful Offline Fallback

Flawless zero-error execution in air-gapped environments via deterministic heuristic simulation when API keys are absent.
